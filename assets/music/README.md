# Music

Audio files are not committed (size + licensing). `library.json` keeps titles, artists, groups and licence info.

Add your own tracks here (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`) or upload them from the app. Good royalty-free sources:

- **Mixkit** (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/) — free, no attribution needed
- **Kevin MacLeod / incompetech** — free, CC BY 4.0, **credit required**
